About

People. Policy. Digital

I work at the intersection of people, society and the digital world.

My work brings together social research, policy analysis, social impact communication, digital safety, web design and UX research.

I explore how social, cultural and technological changes shape everyday life, with interests ranging from gender, masculinities and youth identity to recreation, third spaces, digital culture and technology-facilitated gender-based violence.

Across these areas, I bring research and ideas into practice through policy work, articles, awareness resources, websites and user-centred digital experiences.
